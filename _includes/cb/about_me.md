## About Me
I am a PhD Candidate at UBC Okanagan, in the Interdisciplinary Graduate Studies Program -- Community Engagement, Social Change, and Equity theme. My research explores the role of digital technology in supporting land-based approaches to language revitalization. Through this research, I hope to shed light on some of the ways in which digital tools and spaces for language learning can be used to support and strengthen relationships with the land, and what these tools and spaces might look like. 
As a sessional instructor at UBC Okanagan, I primarily teach courses in linguistic anthropology, such as "Anthropology of Reading and Writing" (ANTH 277) and "Introduction to Linguistic Anthropology" (ANTH 170). 
I am a Canadian settler with mixed European heritage, currently living on unceded lək̓ʷəŋən territory on Vancouver Island, British Columbia.
